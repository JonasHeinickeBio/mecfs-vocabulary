MECFS knowledge graph vocabulary

The document graph's vocabulary

Generated from src/mecfs_kg/schemas/document_graph_vocabulary.py by python -m mecfs_kg.schemas.generate_document_graph; do not edit by hand. The same module renders the OWL declarations (aid-pais-bibliometrics.ttl) and the LinkML attributes (aid-pais-document-metadata.yaml, evidence.yaml); tests/unit/test_vocabulary_parity_unit.py fails if any of the three differs from what the graph writes. Design and numbers: DOCUMENT_METADATA_KG.md.

Namespace aid-pais: = https://w3id.org/mecfs/kg/. A property with another prefix is a standard vocabulary term the graph reuses.

Classes

Class What it is
aid-pais:Authorship One person's position on one paper, with institutions and affiliation text.
aid-pais:MeshHeading A MeSH descriptor assigned to a paper, shared by every paper that carries it.
aid-pais:Topic An OpenAlex topic assigned to a paper.
aid-pais:Grant A funding award, shared by every paper that acknowledges it.
aid-pais:Funder A funding agency, one node per name folded for case and punctuation.
aid-pais:Keyword An author keyword, lower-cased and shared by every paper that uses it (labelled with the corpus's most common spelling).
aid-pais:DocumentSection One section of a paper's parsed full text, described but not quoted: heading, path, kind and sizes. The text is kept in a side store.
aid-pais:DocumentTable One table of a paper's full text: caption, shape and column headers. The cell grid is kept in a side store.
aid-pais:ResourceReference A trial or review registration, code or data repository, accession number, named cohort or classification code a paper's text names.
aid-pais:ExternalWork A work a paper cites that is not a paper of the corpus: DOI and/or PMID and year only.
aid-pais:CitationContext The sentence around an in-text citation of a corpus paper, with the section it sits in.
aid-pais:RecordRelation A Europe PMC link between records: preprint and article, erratum, retraction, expression of concern, comment, update, version.
aid-pais:IdentityLink Why two records of the corpus are linked as the same study or as possibly the same paper: the kind of link and the reason.
aid-pais:Substance A chemical substance a paper is indexed with (MeSH substance or supplementary concept), with its CAS/EC registry number when it has one.
aid-pais:DiscoverySource A place papers are discovered: Europe PMC, PubMed, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar.
aid-pais:SourceHit One discovery source's record of a paper: the source, the id it uses for the paper, and the kind of record it returned.
aid-pais:FullTextLink A place Europe PMC lists where a paper's full text can be read, with its document style and access.
aid-pais:OpenAccessLocation A copy of a paper Unpaywall found: publisher or repository, with version, licence.
aid-pais:TextMinedTerm A concept Europe PMC / PubTator3 text mining found in a paper (disease, gene, chemical, organism, symptom, ...), shared across papers.
aid-pais:TermOccurrence How often, and in which sections, one paper mentions a text-mined term; written for terms mentioned often enough to matter.
